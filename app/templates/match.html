How to read this page
  • MVP: Valve's MVP / Key Players. A dot on the badge means our model picked the same; otherwise its pick is noted below the badge.
  • Stats: raw value, and below it the weighted hero-relative z-score it adds to the score (hover for the hero mean and SD). {% if snapshot %}Baselines: {{ snapshot.n_matches|num }} matches, {{ snapshot.window_start|dt }} → {{ snapshot.window_end|dt }}.{% else %}No baseline snapshot yet, so scores are blank.{% endif %} How the score works.
  • Rank: the player's rank going into the match (ranked matches), else their rank looked up right after it, else their rank now (“now”).
  • On hero: games on this hero before this match and the share won (not-scored ones left out). Tracked players' come from this site's own stored history, as do anyone's this site has more matches of than deadlock-api. Those marked * are counted from the matches deadlock-api has collected, which for most players is far from all: a rough sample, not a record.
  • Party letters (likely parties): Valve's match file doesn't record who queued together, so teammates who were Steam friends before the match started are shown as one party. Only public friend lists are known ({{ friends_known or 0 }} of {{ players|length }} here): a party where nobody's list is public is missed, and friends who were matched by chance would show as one.
  • Names, ranks, friend lists and hero stats come from deadlock-api and Steam; looking them up costs none of the bot's Deadlock budget.
