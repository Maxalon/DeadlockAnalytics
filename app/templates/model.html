How the MVP score works

Valve ranks players by a weighted sum of hero-relative z-scores: for each stat, how far your match total is from the average for your hero, in standard deviations. Values are raw match totals, not per minute.

  • MVP is the highest score on the winning team.
  • Key Players (ranks 2–3) are the next two scores from either team; at least one player from the losing team always makes the top 3.
